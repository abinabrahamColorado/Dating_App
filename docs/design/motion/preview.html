What motion never does here

          
    
            
  • 
              Nothing moves sideways on a decision. Yes and no leave the same quiet way, so neither
              feels like a swipe.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              No confetti, hearts, bounces, shakes, counters ticking up, or sound. The mutual screen
              is the only warm moment and it plays once.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Nothing loops to hold attention. The loading pulse is the only repeating motion and it
              stops the moment content arrives.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Motion only follows a real change of state: something arrived, you decided, a plan was
              confirmed. Nothing animates to invite another tap.
            
    • 
            
  • Nothing takes longer than 600ms, and no animation blocks a tap while it plays.
    • 
            
  • 
              With reduced motion on, positions jump and only opacity fades, in 150ms or less.
